# Attribution and licences

## Original work (this project)
The Astral Bow, the arrow, the floating mountain, the crystal targets, the sky, the clouds and the altar are original and procedural. So are all textures: the filigree, the lotus motifs and the abstract script-like ornament, which spells nothing. All sound effects, the live audio engine and the reel's score (`analysis/astra_music.py`) are synthesized from scratch. There are no samples, stock assets, models or third-party images.

## Music for Instagram
`output/reel_nomusic.mp4` has the voice and the foley only. If you add a commercial track, such as the "Aarambh Hai Prachand" clip the brief suggests, add it through Instagram's licensed music library in the app. It is not included in any file here. `output/beat-grid.json` lists the impact times, so you can line the track's accents up with them.
