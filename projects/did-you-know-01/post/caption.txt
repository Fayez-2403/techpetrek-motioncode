Did you know… computer ko 0.1 + 0.2 bhi nahi aata? 🤯 Aur 2038 mein kuch computers ki ghadi 1901 pe chali jaayegi!

4 tech sach, logic + proof ke saath (sab hamare laptop pe run kiya):

1️⃣ 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004. Binary mein 0.1 kabhi khatam nahi hota (jaise 1/3 = 0.333…), toh round hota hai. Paise ka hisaab float mein mat karo.
2️⃣ Achhi websites tumhara password store nahi karti, sirf uska HASH. Ek letter badla, poora hash alag (64 mein se sirf 6 same).
3️⃣ AI tokens padhta hai: ek hi line English mein 10 tokens, Hindi mein GPT-4 tokenizer pe 52, GPT-4o pe sirf 15 🇮🇳
4️⃣ 32-bit Unix time ki limit: 2,147,483,647 seconds = 19 Jan 2038, 03:14:07 UTC. Agle second → 13 Dec 1901! Fix: 64-bit (292 billion saal)
