For your first drive, start with Three.js. These are the five reference repos I mentioned.

Three.js for the city and vehicles
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
GSAP for animation and camera timing
https://github.com/greensock/GSAP
Remotion for editable video timelines
https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion
faster-whisper for caption timing
https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper
FFmpeg for the final video and audio mix
https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg

You’ll find the reel-pipeline and motion-studio skills here.
https://github.com/Fayez-2403/techpetrek-motioncode/tree/main/.claude/skills
