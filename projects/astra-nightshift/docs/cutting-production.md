# Aakhri Cutting

The 24.8-second revision uses a fictional Mumbai street. The lit cinema, passing rickshaw, station bridge, cabin and tea stall are original illustrations. Separate vehicle, train, windshield-wiper, rain, pouring-tea and steam layers create the movement. No part of Meng To’s video or its audio is used in the cut.

| Seconds | Picture and dialogue |
|---|---|
| 0–3.5 | A silver coupe outside the cinema. A friend asks about one last cutting chai. |
| 3.5–7 | Inside the cabin, the driver knows a place. |
| 7–11.5 | A Mumbai local crosses the bridge. The passenger wonders if the stall is still open. |
| 11.5–15.5 | An amber-lit lane reveals their destination. |
| 15.5–19.5 | A chaiwala pours while the passenger orders two, one with extra ginger. |
| 19.5–24.8 | A shop receipt carries the name GPT-6.2 Astra and a Hinglish prompt for a driving game. |

Amogh voices the passenger and Geet voices the driver in Hindi through ElevenLabs multilingual v2. The character timings place the Roman Hinglish subtitles. Original music uses a plucked-string figure, hand percussion and an electronic bass. Rain, railway wheels, distant horns, glass and pouring Foley were synthesized for the picture. Techpetrek supplied the corner logo. Original project art was created with ImageGen. Tiro Devanagari Hindi, Inter, Archivo and JetBrains Mono are bundled with their font licenses in public/fonts.
