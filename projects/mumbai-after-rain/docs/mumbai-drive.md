# Mumbai After Rain

The 56-second reel was captured from a playable Three.js game. Its garage selection and night drive draw on [Meng To’s reference](https://x.com/MengTo/status/2124015852592988367).

GPT-6.2 Astra authored the Mumbai architecture and lettering, the coupe and traffic models, and the driving simulation and replay camera. The circuit connects fictionalized versions of Mumbai landmarks. The facades, illustrated film poster, market stalls, vehicles, asphalt, rain and reflections all render locally from code. The edit adds Techpetrek’s existing logo, an ElevenLabs voice, synchronized captions, and a synthesized score, engine and tyre sounds.

The review file is 720 × 1280 at 30 fps. The encoded video contains 1,680 frames, 48 kHz AAC audio at −14.0 LUFS and BT.709 video. The Techpetrek logo was matched in every frame. The brightness check detected zero one-frame strobes. The project’s QA script passed seven of eight checks, with the eighth reserved for a 1080 × 1920 delivery master.
