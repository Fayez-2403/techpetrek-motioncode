## Used
- **The filing:** S1 says Anthropic, PBC filed "a registration statement on Form S-1 with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission" for a proposed IPO of Class A common stock. It intends to list on the "Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol 'ANT'". The number of shares and the price range "have not yet been determined". The filing "has not yet become effective".
- **Prospectus contacts:** Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC are named for the preliminary prospectus (S1). The reel says "Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley" and does not label them underwriters.
- **Revenue:** $16.3 billion for the six months ended June 2026, against $4.6 billion for full-year 2025 (S3, citing the S-1).
- **Spending:** "upward of $430 billion on locked-in cloud computing deals" with companies including Amazon and Google (S3).
- **Loss:** a net loss of $80.7 billion through June, "the vast majority of that was the result of accounting charges" (S3).
- **India office:** Bengaluru opened in Feb 2026. It is Anthropic's second office in Asia, after Tokyo, and is led by MD Irina Ghose (S5).
- **India as a market:** the company calls India "the second-largest market for its Claude AI platform", and says its India run-rate revenue has doubled since October 2025 (S5).
- **Indian languages:** a company-wide effort on 10 Indian languages, including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Urdu (S5).
- **Indian partners:** Air India, CRED, Razorpay and Cognizant, among others (S5).
- **Hiring:** the office "will recruit across technical, research and business roles" (S5).
- **Investing from India:**
  - Residents can invest abroad under LRS, up to $250k per financial year (S7).
  - Getting an IPO allotment at the offer price from India is generally hard; most investors buy after listing (S7 explainers).
