Cloudflare ki 26 Oct 2026 report ke mutabik, GLM-5.3 (Workers AI) par 27 Aug se 9 Sep ke beech 155 responses mein doosre customers ki requests ka text aa gaya.

🔹 Sirf extremely long prompts (681,280+ tokens) par hua
🔹 Root cause: KV-cache pool context window se chhota tha + SGLang ka rejection-path bug (rejected request decode tak pahunch gayi aur stale cache pages padh liye)
🔹 Fix: pool ~2.04M tokens tak bada kiya, SGLang patch kiya; 9 Sep ko model wapas live
🔹 Deliberate exploitation ka koi evidence nahi; baaki Workers AI models affected nahi the

Developers: disaggregated SGLang chalate ho toh v0.5.22+ par update karo, aur multi-tenant AI mein cross-tenant isolation tests zaroor rakho.
