🔹 Sirf extremely long prompts par hua (6,81,280+ tokens)
🔹 Root cause: KV-cache pool context window se chhota tha + SGLang ka rejection-path bug
🔹 Reject hui request bhi decode tak gayi aur purane cache pages padh liye
🔹 Fix: pool ~2.04M tokens tak bada kiya, SGLang patch kiya, 9 Sep ko model wapas live
🔹 Deliberate exploitation ka koi evidence nahi mila; baaki Workers AI models affected nahi the

👨‍💻 Developers: disaggregated SGLang chalate ho toh v0.5.22+ par update karo, aur multi-tenant AI mein cross-tenant isolation tests zaroor rakho.
