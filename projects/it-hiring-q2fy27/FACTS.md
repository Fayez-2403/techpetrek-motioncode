## Claims used
- Net headcount change, Q2 FY27:
  - HCLTech **+6,450** (S1).
  - Tech Mahindra **+5,446** (S3).
  - Infosys **+4,480** (S1; 332,542 at Sep 30).
  - TCS **+4,258** (S1: 593,798 → 598,056).
  - Wipro **−6,716** (S1).
- **Sum +13,918** (arithmetic checked; matches the S4 headline).
- Previous quarter (Q1 FY27) combined: **+5,480** (S6 sums to it). 13,918 / 5,480 = 2.54, so the screen says "2.5x".
- Infosys CEO Salil Parekh: "8,000 in the quarter and 20,000 for the full year." CFO: "about 13,000 freshers in H1, in line with our target of approximately 20,000 for FY 2027" (S2). The reel says "around 8,000 college graduates" and "hire karne ki baat kahi".
- HCLTech "recruited 6,136 freshers in the September quarter" (S1). The reel says "recruit kiye", following the source; the brief's "onboarded" was not used.
- Fresher intake numbers are **gross** hires. They are not the net headcount change and not the number of vacancies.

## Not claimed
- 13,918 vacancies or freshers, an India-wide boom, guaranteed jobs, salary changes.
- Viral or trending status. Coverage is several business/HR outlets from Oct 24–26; social numbers are not verified.
- Tech Mahindra's management linked part of its rise to employee transfers from a large deal (IndiaIPO snippet). This is not verified at the primary source, so it is left out.
