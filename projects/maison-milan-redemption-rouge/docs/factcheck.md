## Conflicts found (nothing below is on screen)
- **Volume:**
  - The website title says **100 ml**.
  - The box in both official photos says **90 ML / 3.04 FL OZ**, 80 % vol.
  - The film shows no volume. The brand should settle this before a caption states it.
- **Gender:**
  - The website files it under **Unisex**.
  - The box says "an **oud & raspberry** fragrance **for women**".
  - The film shows no gender. Oud and raspberry are not in the notes list, so we leave them out too.
- **Where to buy:**
  - The Amazon.in seller store (A36L6SNLA0N7RQ) listed 15 products on 29 Oct 2026.
  - **Redemption Rouge was not among them** (checked every page, plus a search by name).
  - The CTA is the website only (the user's choice).
- **Price:** AED 299 → AED 149 (sale) on maisonmilan.co. It's not on screen (the user's choice).
