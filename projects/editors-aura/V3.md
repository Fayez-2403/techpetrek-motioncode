## Honest notes
- **The cracks** are motion graphics on the AI backshot: a head-region warp, plus a punch and a burst on the hands. The clip itself has no such motion.
- **The bot** is an illustration of Claude at work, not a feature of Claude Code.
- **The skill chips** name skills that are installed in this repo; showing them attached to one prompt is a staged composite of the brief.
