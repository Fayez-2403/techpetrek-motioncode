## The music mathematics (new in V4; numbers in `data/aura_v4/song_map.json` → `math`)
1. **The accent phase.** The riddim's accents (the downbeat kick, and the backbeat on 2 and 4) were measured at 1 ms on a causal envelope.
   - They land **30.0 ms before** the 95.002 BPM grid that V1-V3 used (n = 24, IQR −34 to −18 ms). That is two frames at 60 fps, so every hit in V1-V3 trailed its drum.
   - V4 reads the song 30 ms early, so the accents sound on the frame they are drawn.
   - Check: the final slam measures at 20.211 s against the grid's 20.210 s.
2. **Onsets at 1 ms.** Kick, snare and hat onsets come from a 2 ms RMS slope (V1-V3 used 10 ms frames).
   - Every cut and punch is snapped to the nearest measured drum within ±25 ms.
   - Six of the nine run cuts sit within 1 ms of the grid; three moved one frame (16-17 ms) to their own hit.
   - The render choreography keeps its grid anchor, so a V1 render still lands on its own bars.
3. **The bass drop-outs were measured, not assumed.** V1's grid anchors put them up to 0.23 s off.
   - The sub band drops out at **17.873-18.853 s** ("going under") and again at **19.621-20.211 s** ("number one").
   - The pit stop is slow motion (×0.35) and under water while the bass is out. It **launches on the measured bass return** (18.853 s) and freezes through the last gap.
4. **Caption syllables.** Whisper's word starts are snapped to the nearest vocal-band onset within 60 ms (median shift 15.6 ms).
5. **The splice.** The bar-28 and bar-32 downbeats are cross-correlated (+2 ms), then joined with a 12 ms equal-power crossfade.

## The two new renders (light mode)
- **R08 · INK** (`aura/r8_ink.ts`, one shader). Cream paper.
  - An ensō paints itself across the beat: wet at the start, a splitting dry brush at the end.
  - Every measured kick drops ink that blooms with watercolour edge-darkening, granulation, multiply mixing and a wet sheen.
  - Snares throw splatter, and a red chop is stamped on a snare.
- **R09 · BOUNCE** (`aura/r9_bounce.ts`, Three.js). An infinite white studio of glossy pastel toys.
  - Every landing is a measured hit: the sphere and cube on kicks, the capsule and torus on snares, the small ones on hats.
  - Parabolas land exactly on the hit, with squash and stretch that keep volume. The cube flips one face per flight.
  - Each landing ripples a ring across the floor; soft shadows and contact shadows ground the toys.

## The captions (`aura/lyrics_v4.ts`: the song's words, acted out)
- **LIKE A / NUCLEAR / BOMB:** a radiation trefoil, then "BOMB" blows the letters apart with shockwaves and speed lines over the eruption.
- **TALK TO THE / BOYS · GIRLS · WOMAN · MAN:** a speech bubble that pops on every "talk", and pictogram people per noun, then the whole crowd.
- **talk to the → COMPUTER → TONGUE:** typed into a terminal; "TONGUE" decodes from glyphs.
- **GO TELL THEM… TOUGHER / THAN A / LION:** claw slashes split the word, the frame tears open, and a gold LION roars (shake).

- **NUMBER / 1:** a gold 1 with a glint and rays, over the desaturated freeze.
