## Why the first cut was 51.6 s, and how V2 gets to 89 s
- **The first cut.** Dubplate's climax starts at song 2:36.5, and the song fades at 3:18. Climax-first left 41.5 s of song after a 10.1 s intro.
- **V2.** Dubplate is **rearranged on its own measured phrase lines**. It is a constant 95.00 BPM, and every 4-bar phrase opens with the same bass slam, so each join lands on a bar line of the same grid. All of V2's bar lines stay at n x 2.526 s.

**How each join is treated:**
- **A → B:** A's last words ("...young generation") cross its bar line. A's tail rings 0.55 s under B's slam, high-passed so only the vocal carries.
- **B → C:** a 30 ms crossfade. Each join was measured, and the level jumps into the next phrase's slam with no dip.

**Posting.** A rearranged song can't come from Instagram's library, so the audio has to be baked into the upload. Two commercial songs in the upload risk the reel being muted, limited or blocked (PLAN.md, "Posting with two songs").
